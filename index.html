About EarthTreats

Built for Campus Sustainability

EarthTreats is an Android-exclusive recycling platform designed to motivate students and staff to recycle paper, plastic bottles, and aluminum cans. Unlike static recycling initiatives, EarthTreats integrates live GPS station mapping, QR scan validation, and an in-app reward point shop to deliver a seamless eco-conscious experience.
